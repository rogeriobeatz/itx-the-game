# Privacy and fictional cast policy

This project is a fictional corporate satire. All characters, money, bitcoin, compliance and investigation mechanics are fictional game devices and do not refer to particular people or allegations.

## Mandatory rules
- Never use the real first name, surname, recognizable nickname or other personal identifier of an employee, colleague, executive, speaker or third party in **any** character, dialogue, quest, asset, spreadsheet, screenshot caption or published copy.
- Use only approved role-based fictional character aliases. Before adding a new NPC, record a fictional role in the project tracker and update the cast validation test.
- Do not reproduce recognizable portraits or claim misconduct by identifiable individuals. Review existing portrait illustrations before using them for promotion.
- Generic environmental details from photography are artistic references. Do not claim the game maps reproduce the real building blueprint.
- Git history is synchronized with the design platform. Avoid history-rewriting operations without a separately reviewed migration plan; old revisions may remain visible and need their own remediation strategy.
